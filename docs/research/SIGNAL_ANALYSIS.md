# SIGNAL ANALYSIS
## Forex Neural Trading Engine — Signal Generation & Alpha Decay

This document examines the lifecycle of alpha signals generated by the neural ensemble, their predictive decay profiles, and their impact on execution strategy.

---

## 1. The AlphaSignal Object

All models within the ensemble are tasked with producing continuous predictions that are eventually mapped and thresholded into an `AlphaSignal`.

The final aggregated `AlphaSignal` contains:
* **Direction**: Long (+1), Flat (0), Short (-1).
* **Magnitude**: Scaling factor $[0, 1]$ used as a base multiplier for position sizing.
* **Confidence vs Uncertainty**: The model outputs a base confidence, but the Monte Carlo Dropout layer measures true epistemic uncertainty. High uncertainty actively suppresses the signal's effective weight in the Bayesian Model Averager (BMA).
* **Expected Decay Steps**: Estimate of the signal's half-life (how long the alpha will persist before being arbitraged away or absorbed by market noise).

---

## 2. Signal Decay & Half-Life

A critical research finding is that signals derived from different feature subsets exhibit vastly different decay profiles:

### Microstructure Signals (Fast Decay)
* **Features**: Order book imbalance, VPIN, Amihud illiquidity.
* **Alpha Horizon**: $< 5$ minutes.
* **Execution Constraint**: Must be executed aggressively using Market orders. Limit orders suffer extreme adverse selection (they are only filled when the alpha signal is wrong).

### Macro / Sentiment Signals (Slow Decay)
* **Features**: COT, FinBERT, Yield Differentials.
* **Alpha Horizon**: Days to Weeks.
* **Execution Constraint**: Easily executed passively via Iceberg or VWAP routers to capture the spread, as the signal will persist long enough to survive patient order filling.

### Model Integration
The `EnsembleAggregator` inherently attempts to blend these horizons. If the LightGBM meta-model detects high short-term volatility, it naturally up-weights the Temporal Model (TCN) trained on microstructure features, instructing the `ExecutionEngine` to employ aggressive TWAP execution profiles.

---

## 3. Signal Filtering & Gating

Before a signal reaches execution, it undergoes a rigid filtering process in the Risk Engine:
1. **Regime Alignment Check**: A strong mean-reversion signal generated during a `High Volatility Trending` regime is heavily penalized or outright discarded.
2. **Correlation Gate**: If the portfolio already holds maximum long USD exposure (e.g., Short EUR/USD, Short GBP/USD), a new Long USD/JPY signal is gated to prevent systematic correlation blowups.
3. **Volatility Scaling (Fractional Kelly)**: The optimal bet size is calculated using $f^* = \frac{\mu}{\sigma^2}$. Since neural network probability estimates are notoriously overconfident, we scale this down (e.g., $0.25$ Kelly factor) to ensure long-term portfolio survival.
