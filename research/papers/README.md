# Research Papers Index

## Essential Reading

### Market Microstructure
- **Easley et al. (2012)** — "Flow Toxicity and Liquidity in a High Frequency World" — VPIN
- **Kyle (1985)** — "Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading" — Kyle's lambda
- **Amihud (2002)** — "Illiquidity and Stock Returns" — Amihud ratio
- **Glosten & Milgrom (1985)** — "Bid, Ask and Transaction Prices" — spread decomposition

### Statistical Signal Processing
- **Hurst (1951)** — "Long-term Storage Capacity of Reservoirs" — Hurst exponent
- **Kalman (1960)** — "A New Approach to Linear Filtering" — Kalman filter
- **Mallat (1989)** — "A Theory for Multiresolution Signal Decomposition" — Wavelets

### Machine Learning for Trading
- **Finn et al. (2017)** — "Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning" — MAML
- **Schulman et al. (2017)** — "Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms" — PPO
- **Haarnoja et al. (2018)** — "Soft Actor-Critic" — SAC
- **Bai et al. (2018)** — "An Empirical Evaluation of Generic Convolutional and Recurrent Networks" — TCN

### Quantitative Finance
- **Kelly (1956)** — "A New Interpretation of Information Rate" — Kelly criterion
- **Sharpe (1966)** — "Mutual Fund Performance" — Sharpe ratio
- **Rockafellar & Uryasev (2000)** — "Optimization of Conditional Value-at-Risk" — CVaR
- **Lopez de Prado (2018)** — "Advances in Financial Machine Learning" — Book (essential)

### Regime Detection
- **Hamilton (1989)** — "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series" — Markov switching
- **Baum et al. (1970)** — "A Maximization Technique in HMM" — Baum-Welch
